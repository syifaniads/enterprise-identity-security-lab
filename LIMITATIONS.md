- Capstone 1 is a collaborative team artifact; exact per-person implementation ownership is not fully reconstructable from retained metadata.
- Stack #3 IAM training material confirms curriculum coverage but the retained tracker does not directly assign that page to Syifani.

- IdentityIQ integration in this repository is an architecture/pseudoconfiguration artifact, not a runnable SailPoint deployment.
- IAM shift-roster data is treated as an operations-planning exercise, not proof of employment or production operations.
- Policy values from the 2025 lab are historical exercise values and should not be transplanted into production without risk analysis, compatibility testing, and current organizational standards.
