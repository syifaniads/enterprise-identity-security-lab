## Evidence levels

**Direct** — revision or assignment metadata explicitly ties the artifact to Syifani.

**Team** — retained artifact proves the team performed/documented the work, but does not isolate Syifani's exact implementation contribution.

**Context** — curriculum or training material proves subject exposure but is not by itself project execution evidence.

**Extension** — created in 2026 to make the portfolio reproducible, safer, and easier for senior reviewers to inspect.

## Source handling policy

The public repository does not include:

- original course/vendor PDFs;
- private Google/Notion/Canva/SharePoint edit URLs;
- historical passwords or password-bearing CSV files;
- private screenshots;
- student IDs;
- real tenant names, internal IP addresses, session tokens, API keys, private keys, or certificates.
